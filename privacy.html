Privacy Policy

Ingenuity 3D Publishing Tool · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

Ingenuity 3D is an independent educational video channel. This policy covers the private, in-house software we use to publish our own videos and read our own analytics (the "Tool"). The Tool is used only by the owner of the Ingenuity 3D channel.

Which services the Tool uses

  • YouTube API Services: to upload our own videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, post comments on our own videos, and read our own channel's analytics. By using YouTube features of the Tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Graph API (Facebook and Instagram): to publish our own Reels to our own Facebook Page and Instagram account, and read their insights. Meta's handling of data is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

What data the Tool accesses

  • Only data belonging to the Ingenuity 3D channel and accounts: our videos and their metadata, our channel and account statistics (views, watch time, retention, reach, likes, shares, saves, followers, aggregated and anonymous audience breakdowns such as country or age group), and the text of public comments on our own videos.
  • The Tool does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube, Facebook or Instagram user's account, and it does not sign in other users.

How the data is used

Solely to publish our own content and to understand how our videos perform so we can make better videos. We do not sell, rent, share or transfer this data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling of individuals.

Storage and security

  • Data and access tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in files excluded from any public code repository.
  • Analytics snapshots are kept for as long as they are useful for comparing our own videos and can be deleted at any time.
  • Authorized YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Data deletion

  • Stored YouTube API data (analytics snapshots and comment text of our own videos) is deleted or refreshed within 30 days.
  • The channel owner can delete all stored data and tokens at any time by deleting the tool's local data and token files; revoking access (below) stops all further access immediately.
  • To request deletion of any data related to you, email the contact address below; we respond within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access granted to the Tool can be revoked at any time: Google/YouTube via Google security settings (third-party access); Facebook and Instagram via Meta Business Settings or Business Integrations.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.
